What we do

Full-service grooming, done gently

From a complete breed-style groom to a quick walk-in nail trim, every dog gets careful, patient attention — and plenty of treats along the way.

Full Grooms & Breed Cuts

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out and a clean, comfortable cut styled to suit your dog's breed and coat — start to finish, done right.

Walk-In Nail Trims

Quick, low-stress nail trims — many pups are in and out in minutes. Drop by on your way past; no appointment needed for a quick trim.

Baths, Brush-Outs & De-Shed

A warm, sudsy bath with a thorough brush-out and de-shedding treatment to leave your dog's coat soft, clean and tangle-free.

Puppy's First Groom

Gentle, reassuring first-timers welcome — even 10-week-old puppies. We keep it calm and positive so grooming feels like a good thing.

Calm, One-on-One Handling

Spotless, Unhurried Salon
